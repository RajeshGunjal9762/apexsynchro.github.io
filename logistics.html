What We Handle

End-to-End Export Capabilities

Multi-Modal Freight

Air, sea, and land freight options tailored to your urgency and volume. We work with established freight forwarders on all major trade lanes to ensure competitive rates and reliable transit times.

Export Documentation

Full export documentation prepared and verified for every shipment: commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, health certificates, and country-specific import permits.

Customs Clearance Support

We coordinate with licensed customs brokers in destination countries to ensure smooth clearance. HS code classification, duty optimization, and import licensing guidance included.

Cold-Chain Compliance

Temperature-sensitive products are handled under validated cold-chain protocols. Refrigerated containers, temperature loggers, and GDP-compliant packaging available on request.

Track & Trace

Real-time shipment tracking from origin to destination. Dedicated account managers provide proactive updates at every milestone — departure, transit, customs, and delivery.

Regulatory Pre-Clearance

We pre-screen all shipments against destination country import regulations for medical devices and consumables — preventing costly delays at the border before they happen.

Trade Terms

Incoterms We Support

We accommodate a full range of Incoterms to match your procurement model and risk appetite. Our trade team will recommend the most appropriate terms for your destination and volume.

EXW
Ex Works

Buyer assumes all transport and risk from our facility. Suitable for buyers with established freight arrangements.

FOB
Free On Board

We deliver goods to the named port of shipment. Risk transfers to buyer once goods are on board the vessel.

CIF
Cost, Insurance & Freight

We cover cost, insurance, and freight to the destination port. Buyer handles import customs and inland delivery.

DAP
Delivered At Place

We deliver to the named destination, ready for unloading. Buyer handles import duties and taxes.

DDP
Delivered Duty Paid Most Popular

Full door-to-door service. We handle all freight, insurance, customs, duties, and delivery to your facility.
