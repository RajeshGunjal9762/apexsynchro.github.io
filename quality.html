Our Certifications

Internationally Recognised Standards

Every certification we hold is actively maintained through regular audits, renewals, and ongoing compliance monitoring — not a one-time achievement.

ISO 9001

Quality Management System

Issued by: International Organization for Standardization

ISO 9001 certification confirms that our quality management system consistently meets customer and regulatory requirements. Every process — from supplier qualification to shipment release — is governed by documented procedures and subject to regular internal and third-party audits.

Scope Applies to all procurement, quality control, and logistics operations across Apex Synchro.

CE Mark

European Conformity

Issued by: European Commission

CE marking confirms that our products meet EU health, safety, and environmental protection requirements under the Medical Devices Regulation (MDR 2017/745). All CE-marked products are assessed by a Notified Body and carry a Declaration of Conformity.

Scope Covers all surgical glove and medical wipe product lines exported to the European Economic Area.

WHO GMP

Good Manufacturing Practice

Issued by: World Health Organization

WHO GMP compliance ensures that products are consistently produced and controlled according to quality standards appropriate for their intended use. This certification is required for supply to UN agencies, NGOs, and government health programmes in developing markets.

Scope Applies to medical wipes product range and all products supplied to WHO-affiliated procurement programmes.

FDA

FDA Registered Facility

Issued by: U.S. Food & Drug Administration

Our manufacturing partners hold FDA 510(k) clearance for surgical glove product lines. Apex Synchro is registered as an importer of record for FDA-regulated medical devices, ensuring full compliance with 21 CFR Part 820 quality system regulations.

Scope Covers surgical rubber glove product lines exported to the United States market.

EN 455

Medical Gloves Standard

Issued by: European Committee for Standardization

EN 455 is the European standard for medical gloves for single use, covering freedom from holes (Part 1), physical properties (Part 2), biological evaluation (Part 3), and shelf life requirements (Part 4). All gloves products meet or exceed EN 455 requirements.

Scope Applies to all surgical and examination glove product lines.

EN 14476

Virucidal Activity Standard

Issued by: European Committee for Standardization

EN 14476 quantitative suspension test confirms virucidal activity of chemical disinfectants and antiseptics used in the medical area. All disinfectant wipe products demonstrate efficacy against enveloped and non-enveloped viruses including SARS-CoV-2.

Scope Applies to surface disinfectant and antiseptic wipe product lines.

How We Ensure Quality

Our Quality Control Process

A multi-stage quality control framework governs every product that passes through the Apex Synchro supply chain — from initial manufacturer qualification through to final shipment release.

Third-party inspection available on request

01

Manufacturer Qualification

All manufacturing partners undergo a rigorous qualification process before onboarding. We audit facilities against ISO 9001, GMP, and relevant product standards — reviewing production processes, quality systems, and regulatory certifications. Only qualified manufacturers enter our approved supplier list.

02

Pre-Production Specification Review

Before each production run, our quality team reviews and approves product specifications, raw material certificates, and production parameters. Any deviation from approved specifications requires formal change control approval before production proceeds.

03

In-Process Quality Monitoring

Manufacturers conduct in-process quality checks at defined intervals during production. Critical parameters — dimensions, tensile strength, AQL sampling, sterility indicators — are monitored and recorded. Apex Synchro receives real-time production reports for high-volume orders.

04

Pre-Shipment Inspection

Every batch undergoes a pre-shipment inspection before release. This includes AQL sampling to ISO 2859-1, physical property verification, labelling and packaging checks, and documentation review. Third-party inspection by SGS, Bureau Veritas, or equivalent is available on request.

05

Documentation Verification

Our compliance team verifies the complete documentation package for every shipment: test reports, certificates of analysis, certificates of conformity, and regulatory certificates. No shipment is released without a complete and verified documentation set.

06

Post-Delivery Quality Review

We maintain a post-delivery quality review programme, tracking any field complaints or non-conformances reported by clients. All complaints are investigated under our formal CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action) process, with findings fed back into supplier performance reviews.
