15+ Years in Global Trade

Our Story

From Regional Distributor to Global Trade Partner

Apex Synchro was founded on a single conviction: that healthcare supply chains deserve the same precision and reliability as the clinical procedures they support. What began as a regional distribution operation has grown into a globally recognized B2B trade partner, serving clients across Asia, Europe, the Americas, and the Middle East.

Over the years, we have built deep relationships with ISO-certified manufacturers across Southeast Asia and established logistics corridors that span six continents. Our team combines decades of experience in pharmaceutical trade, export compliance, and international logistics.

Today, Apex Synchro manages millions of units annually — from surgical rubber gloves to medical-grade wipes — ensuring that every product that leaves our supply chain meets the exact specifications our clients depend on.
